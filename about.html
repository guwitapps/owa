About Owa Delivery Services

Connecting local food vendors, kitchens, and merchants directly with customers across Badagry, Lagos, Nigeria.

Our Mission

Owa Delivery Services was established to bridge the gap between hard-working local food vendors and customers seeking fast, trustworthy delivery. We provide on-demand food logistics, ensuring meals are picked up fresh and delivered safely to households and offices.

Who We Are

Operating under CAC registered business name Owa Delivery Services, we specialize in food delivery logistics within Badagry, Lagos State. By empowering small and medium vendors with delivery technology and rider networks, we allow local businesses to expand their customer reach seamlessly.
