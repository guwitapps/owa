About us

We move what the day needs.

Owa Delivery Services began by carrying meals from Badagry kitchens. The riders were already on the road, so we started carrying everything else too.

What we do

Owa connects customers with local vendors and delivers the result. That covers cooked food, cooking gas refills, pharmacy and chemist items, groceries and market runs, laundry pick-up and return, point-to-point parcels and errands within our coverage area. You send Hi on WhatsApp, tap your order link, pay only after the shop confirms, and follow the order on its own page until it reaches your door.

Why a web page, WhatsApp and missed calls, not a big app

Most of our customers already have WhatsApp open, and most have limited phone storage and patchy data. Asking them to download an app, create an account and remember a password would lose more people than it served. So you can start with a simple Hi on WhatsApp, the menu is a light web page anyone can open, and your order has its own page you can add to your home screen in one tap.

Data is often off, so when something needs you, Owa gives you a free missed call: it reaches any phone and costs you nothing. And if you have no internet at all, you can order by phone and we flash you from the same number when there is news.

How an order runs

  1. You send Hi on WhatsApp and tap Order now (it logs you in), then pick your area, a service and a vendor.
  2. You add your items and your address. The total updates as you go.
  3. You tap Place order. Your order gets one number, like OWA-K7F3P.
  4. We confirm with the vendor that everything is available, and our team confirms the delivery fee.
  5. We give you a missed call, and the Pay button appears on your order page: card, transfer or USSD.
  6. The vendor prepares it, and a rider in your area collects it.
  7. Your order page shows the rider's name and number, and we flash you when the rider is coming.
  8. You tap I got it when it arrives.

For vendors

Vendors get a missed call for every new order and see it on their own Owa page, confirm availability with one tap, and mark it ready when it is. There is nothing to download from the Play Store and no dashboard to learn. Your menu goes live the same day.

For riders

Riders cover their own areas, so jobs land with someone already nearby. A missed call means a job; the first rider to tap takes it. Every job shows the pickup, the drop-off address, the customer's number and a map button where the customer shared a location. Riders tap Delivered on their Owa page.

Who we are
