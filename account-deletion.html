User Data & Account Deletion

In compliance with Meta Platform Policies and international privacy standards, Owa Delivery Services provides users with the ability to request full deletion of their personal information and order history.

How to Request Data Deletion

To purge your phone number, delivery address, and communication records from our database:

  1. Send an email to info@owadelivery.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your full name and the telephone number used when placing orders on WhatsApp.

Our compliance team will process and confirm your request within 48 to 72 business hours.
