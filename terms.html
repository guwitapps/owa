By ordering through our menu page or our WhatsApp service, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not place an order.

1. What we do

Owa Delivery Services is a local delivery and errand business. We take your order, buy or collect the items from a vendor on your behalf, and deliver them to the address you give us. This covers food, cooking gas, pharmacy and chemist items, groceries and parcels within our published coverage area.

We are an intermediary. The items themselves are prepared or supplied by independent vendors.

2. Placing an order

Orders are built on our menu page, where you choose your area, a service, a vendor and your items. Sending that order opens a WhatsApp conversation with our team, with the order already filled in.

An order is only binding once we have confirmed availability with the vendor and you have received the priced confirmation. Until then, nothing is charged and nothing is committed.

3. Pricing

Every order is quoted in full before payment. Your total is made up of:

LineWhat it is
ItemsThe vendor's own price for what you ordered.
PackagingCharged per pack, where the service uses packs.
Service chargeA percentage of the items and packaging, shown as a percentage in your confirmation.
Delivery feeA flat fee for your zone. Our team may adjust it for unusual trips, and you will see the final figure before paying.

The running total shown on the menu page covers items and packaging only. The service charge and the delivery fee are added when we send your confirmation, and no payment is due before then.

Prices come from the vendor and can change without notice. The figure in your confirmation is the figure you pay.

4. Payment

Payment is by bank transfer to the account shown in your order confirmation. Please quote your order number as the transfer narration and send the receipt to the number given. Preparation begins once our team confirms the payment.

We do not collect card or bank details on this website, and we never ask for your PIN, your one-time password or your full card number.

5. Cancellations

  • Before the vendor starts preparing — cancel free of charge.
  • After preparation has started — perishable and prepared items cannot usually be cancelled, as the vendor has already incurred the cost.
  • If we cannot fulfil your order — because the vendor is unavailable or an item is out of stock — you are not charged, and any payment already made is refunded in full.

If you do not respond to our messages, an unpaid order is closed automatically and nothing is charged.

6. Delivery

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. Traffic, weather and vendor preparation times all affect them. Please make sure someone can receive the order at the address given, and that the phone number you ordered from can be reached.

If nobody is available and the rider cannot reach you, the order may be returned and the delivery fee remains payable.

7. Problems with an order

Tell us within 24 hours of delivery, in the same conversation as the order, quoting your order number. Missing, damaged or incorrect items are resolved case by case, usually by refund or replacement. We may ask for a photograph.

8. Ratings

After delivery you may rate the rider, the item and our service. Ratings are optional, used internally to manage quality, and are not published against your name.

9. Acceptable use

You agree not to use our service for anything illegal, and not to ask riders to carry prohibited, dangerous or restricted goods. We may decline any order at our discretion. Prescription medicines are subject to the pharmacy's own requirements — we cannot bypass them.

Our service is intended for people aged 18 and over.

10. Limits of liability

We are responsible for handling your order carefully in transit. We are not responsible for the quality, preparation or safety of goods produced by a vendor, beyond delivering them as received. Our liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for that order. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited under Nigerian law.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms and will change the date at the top of this page when we do. Continuing to order after a change means you accept the updated terms.

12. Governing law and contact
