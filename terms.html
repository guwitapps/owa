By ordering through our menu, our WhatsApp service or by phone, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not place an order.

1. What we do

Owa Delivery Services is a local delivery and errand business. We take your order, buy or collect the items from a vendor on your behalf, and deliver them to the address you give us. This covers food, cooking gas, pharmacy and chemist items, groceries, laundry, parcels and errands within our published coverage area.

We are an intermediary. The items themselves are prepared or supplied by independent vendors.

2. Placing an order

Orders are placed on our menu (which you can also open from WhatsApp), where you choose your area, a service, a vendor and your items, or by phone with our team. We take orders every day from 7:00am to 8:00pm (Lagos time); we close at 9:00pm.

An order is only binding once we have confirmed availability with the vendor and confirmed the delivery fee, and you have paid. Until then, nothing is charged and nothing is committed.

3. Pricing

Every order is quoted in full before payment. Your total is made up of:

LineWhat it is
ItemsThe vendor's own price for what you ordered.
PackagingCharged per pack, where the service uses packs.
Service chargeA percentage of the items and packaging, shown on your order.
Delivery feeWorked out from the distance when you share your location, or a flat fee for your area. Our team confirms it before you pay, and you see the final figure first.

The total shown when you place an order may include an estimated delivery fee. The final total is the one on your Pay button, and no payment is due before then.

Prices come from the vendor and can change without notice. The total on your Pay button is the figure you pay.

4. Payment

When the order is confirmed, you pay with the Pay button on your order page: card, bank transfer or USSD, processed by Paystack. Payment is due within 30 minutes of the Pay button appearing; the vendor starts preparing once the payment is confirmed. Customers who order by phone without internet pay by bank transfer to Owa's account, and our team confirms it.

Your card details are entered on Paystack's page and never reach us. We never ask for your PIN, your one-time password or your full card number.

Pay on delivery

For some orders you may choose to pay on delivery: goods that do not spoil, or customers with a record of completed, paid orders. It is offered only when the order page shows the choice, for orders up to the limit shown there, and one such order at a time. By choosing it and ticking the box, you agree that:

  • You pay the full total shown when the rider arrives, before the order is handed over, either in cash to an Owa rider who is approved to collect cash, or by transfer to Owa's own bank account shown on your order page. Never pay into a rider's or anyone else's personal account; such a payment is not a payment to Owa. You may also pay online with the Pay button at any time before.
  • You may refuse an order that is wrong, damaged, incomplete or very late; then you pay nothing and we put it right.
  • If you refuse an order without such a reason, or are not reachable at the address you gave, you owe Owa the delivery fee for the trip, and pay on delivery is withdrawn from your number. Owa may recover what is owed through lawful means, including the courts, and may decline future orders until it is paid.
  • Owa may withdraw pay on delivery from any customer or order at any time, without giving a reason.

5. Cancellations

  • Before the vendor starts preparing — cancel free of charge.
  • After preparation has started — perishable and prepared items cannot usually be cancelled, as the vendor has already incurred the cost.
  • If we cannot fulfil your order — because the vendor is unavailable or an item is out of stock — you are not charged, and any payment already made is refunded in full.

An order that is not paid within the payment time is closed automatically and nothing is charged.

6. Delivery

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. Traffic, weather and vendor preparation times all affect them. Please make sure someone can receive the order at the address given, and that the phone number you ordered from can be reached.

If nobody is available and the rider cannot reach you, the order may be returned and the delivery fee remains payable.

7. Problems with an order

Tell us within 24 hours of delivery: tap There is a problem on your order page, or call us, quoting your order number. Missing, damaged or incorrect items are resolved case by case, usually by refund or replacement. We may ask for a photograph.

8. Updates and missed calls

Every update about your order is shown on its order page. When something needs you (it is time to pay, the rider is coming, or the order could not go ahead) we may give you a brief missed call from Owa's alerts number, between 7:00am and 10:00pm. It costs you nothing. Customers who order by phone without internet are flashed from the number they ordered on, and are told the news when they call back.

9. Acceptable use

You agree not to use our service for anything illegal, and not to ask riders to carry prohibited, dangerous or restricted goods. We may decline any order at our discretion. Prescription medicines are subject to the pharmacy's own requirements — we cannot bypass them.

Our service is intended for people aged 18 and over.

10. Limits of liability

We are responsible for handling your order carefully in transit. We are not responsible for the quality, preparation or safety of goods produced by a vendor, beyond delivering them as received. Our liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for that order. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited under Nigerian law.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms and will change the date at the top of this page when we do. Continuing to order after a change means you accept the updated terms.

12. Governing law and contact
