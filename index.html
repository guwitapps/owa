How it works

Choose, pay, and follow it to your door.

Send Hi to Owa on WhatsApp for your order link, or open the menu straight away. Your order page shows every step, and a free missed call from Owa tells you when something needs you.

Step 01

Choose your area and shop

The menu shows only the shops that deliver to your area, and only what they have today. Prices and the delivery fee are shown as you go.

Step 02

Place the order

We check with the shop that everything is available, and our team confirms your delivery fee. You pay nothing until then.

Step 03

Pay when we flash you

A missed call from Owa means it is time to pay. Tap Pay on your order page: card, bank transfer or USSD, through Paystack.

Step 04

Follow it to your door

Your order page shows when the shop is preparing it and who your rider is. We flash you when the rider is coming. Tap I got it when it arrives.

No internet on your phone? Call 0704 164 9942. We take your order on the phone, flash you from the same number when there is news, and you pay by bank transfer.
Before you order

The rules, in plain words.

The short version of our terms of service. Nothing here replaces the full terms — it just puts the parts that affect your order where you can see them before you place one.

What your total is made of

  • The vendor's own price for what you ordered Items
  • Charged per pack, where the service uses packs Packaging
  • A percentage of the items and packaging, shown on your order Service charge
  • From the distance when you share your location, or a flat fee for your area. Our team confirms it before you pay Delivery fee
Prices come from the vendor and can change without notice. The total on your Pay button is the total you pay, and you see it before any money moves.

Nothing is charged until the shop confirms

We first check with the shop that everything is available and confirm your delivery fee. Only then do you get the Pay button. If the shop cannot do it, you are not charged.

Paying

With the Pay button: card, bank transfer or USSD, through Paystack. We never see your card details. Phone orders without internet pay by bank transfer to Owa's account.

Cancelling

Free before the vendor starts preparing. After that, prepared and perishable items usually cannot be cancelled. If we cannot fulfil your order, you are refunded in full.

If something is wrong

Tell us within 24 hours of delivery: tap There is a problem on your order page, or call us, quoting your order number. We resolve it case by case.

We are an intermediary

The items are prepared or supplied by independent vendors. We are responsible for handling your order carefully in transit, not for how a vendor prepared it.

Someone has to receive it

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. If nobody can take the order and the rider cannot reach you, it may be returned and the delivery fee still applies.

Questions

Asked often enough to answer here.

How do I place an order?

Open the menu (or send Hi to Owa on WhatsApp for your own order link), pick your area, a shop and your items, type your address and tap Place order. No smartphone? Call 0704 164 9942 and we take it by phone.

When do I pay, and how?

After the shop confirms it has everything and our team confirms the delivery fee. We give you a missed call, and the Pay button appears on your order page: card, bank transfer or USSD, through Paystack. You have 30 minutes to pay. Phone orders without internet pay by bank transfer, and our team confirms it.

What exactly am I paying for?

Four lines: the vendor's price for the items; packaging, charged per pack where the service uses packs; a service charge, shown as a percentage of the items and packaging; and a delivery fee, from the distance if you shared your location or a flat fee for your area. The full breakdown is on your order before you pay.

What does a missed call from Owa mean?

That you have news about your order: it is time to pay, the rider is coming, or the order could not go ahead. Open your order page (or WhatsApp) to see it. The call costs you nothing, and works even when your data is off.

When are you open?

Every day from 7am to 9pm (Lagos time). The last orders are taken at 8pm, so every order can be delivered before we close.

How long will it take?

Around thirty minutes is typical, but delivery times are estimates rather than guarantees. Traffic, weather and how long the vendor takes to prepare your order all affect them.

Can I cancel?

Free of charge before the vendor starts preparing. Once preparation has started, prepared and perishable items usually cannot be cancelled, because the vendor has already incurred the cost. If we cannot fulfil your order at all, you are not charged and anything already paid is refunded in full.

Something was missing or damaged. What now?

Within 24 hours of delivery, tap There is a problem on your order page or call 0704 164 9942, quoting your order number. We may ask for a photograph. Problems are resolved case by case, usually by refund or replacement.

Do I need to install an app?

No. The menu and your order page work in your phone's browser. If you like, you can add Owa to your home screen in one tap (the page shows you how) and turn on free alerts. Nothing to download from the Play Store, no password.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Sharing your location is optional; a typed address and landmark are enough. If you share it, the delivery fee is worked out from the real distance and the rider finds you faster. Our privacy policy sets out the detail.

Which areas do you cover?

Ajara, Ajido, Apa, Awhanjigoh, Ibereko, Ikoga, Ilogbo-Araromi, Iworo Gbanko, Iya-Afin, Keta-East and Posukoh. The menu only shows areas that have a shop and a rider actually delivering there, so what you see is genuinely available.
