How it works

Two ways in. One chat.

No app to install and no account to create. Pick your items on the website or in the chat — either way the order finishes in WhatsApp and you are told at every handover.

Step 01

Pick your area and vendor

On the menu page you see today's vendors and prices for your area. Prefer to type? Send hi and we'll walk you through the same choices in the chat.

Step 02

Send your order

One tap sends your whole basket to WhatsApp as a single message. We confirm the vendor has everything before you pay anything.

Step 03

Share your location

One tap sends your pin. The delivery fee is worked out from your actual distance, and you see the full price before paying.

Step 04

Track to your door

We message you when it's being prepared, when a rider collects it, and when it arrives.

Coverage

Built for Badagry first.

We run zone by zone rather than claiming the whole state. Each zone has its own delivery rate, and your final fee is worked out from the distance to your door and shown before you pay. If your area isn't listed yet, message us — new zones open as rider coverage grows.
