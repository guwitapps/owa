How it works

Four steps, all inside WhatsApp.

No app to install and no account to create. The order runs as a normal chat, and you are told at every handover.

Step 01

Say hi

Message us and pick what you need — food, gas, pharmacy, groceries or an errand.

Step 02

Choose and confirm

Pick your area, then your vendor and items. We confirm availability before you pay anything.

Step 03

Share your location

One tap sends your pin so the rider finds you first time. You get the full price before paying.

Step 04

Track to your door

We message you when it's being prepared, when a rider collects it, and when it arrives.

Coverage

Built for Badagry first.

We run zone by zone rather than claiming the whole state. Each zone has its own flat delivery fee, shown to you before you pay. If your area isn't listed yet, message us — new zones open as rider coverage grows.

What you'll always get

  • The full price broken down before payment — items, packaging, service and delivery.
  • An order number to quote on your transfer.

  • A chance to rate the rider, the item and the service afterwards.
