How it works

Build it here. Finish it in the chat.

No app to install and no account to create. You choose everything on the menu page, and the finished order arrives on WhatsApp as one message our team can act on straight away.

Step 01

Pick your area and vendor

The menu shows only the vendors that deliver to your area, and only what they have listed today. Your area also sets the delivery rate for the order.

Step 02

Add your items

Tap to add, tap again for more. The running total for items and packaging updates as you go, so nothing about the price is a surprise later.

Step 03

Send the order

One tap sends the whole basket to WhatsApp as a single message. We check with the vendor that everything is available, then send you the full priced confirmation.

Step 04

Pay and track to your door

Pay by transfer quoting your order number. We message you when the vendor starts, when a rider collects it, and when it arrives.

Before you order

The rules, in plain words.

The short version of our terms of service. Nothing here replaces the full terms — it just puts the parts that affect your order where you can see them before you place one.

What your total is made of

  • The vendor's own price for what you ordered Items
  • Charged per pack, where the service uses packs Packaging
  • A percentage of the items and packaging, shown as a percentage in your confirmation Service charge
  • A flat fee for your zone. Our team may adjust it for unusual trips Delivery fee
Prices come from the vendor and can change without notice. The figure in your confirmation is the figure you pay, and you see it before any money moves.

Nothing is charged until you confirm

An order is only binding once we have checked availability with the vendor and you have received the priced confirmation. Until then nothing is charged and nothing is committed.

Payment is by bank transfer

To the account in your confirmation, quoting your order number as the narration. We never ask for card details, and none are collected on this website.

Cancelling

Free before the vendor starts preparing. After that, prepared and perishable items usually cannot be cancelled. If we cannot fulfil your order, you are refunded in full.

If something is wrong

Tell us within 24 hours of delivery, in the same conversation, quoting your order number. Missing, damaged or incorrect items are resolved case by case.

We are an intermediary

The items are prepared or supplied by independent vendors. We are responsible for handling your order carefully in transit, not for how a vendor prepared it.

Someone has to receive it

Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees. If nobody can take the order and the rider cannot reach you, it may be returned and the delivery fee still applies.

Questions

Asked often enough to answer here.

How do I place an order?

Open the menu page, pick your area, choose a service and a vendor, then add your items. When you're finished, one tap sends the whole order to our WhatsApp line as a single message — vendor, area, items and quantities already filled in.

When do I pay, and how?

After we confirm with the vendor that everything is available and send you the priced confirmation. Payment is by bank transfer to the account shown in that confirmation. Quote your order number as the transfer narration and send us the receipt. We do not collect card or bank details on this website.

What exactly am I paying for?

Four lines: the vendor's price for the items; packaging, charged per pack where the service uses packs; a service charge, shown as a percentage of the items and packaging; and a flat delivery fee for your zone, which our team may adjust for unusual trips. The full breakdown is in your confirmation before you transfer anything.

How long will it take?

Around thirty minutes is typical, but delivery times are estimates rather than guarantees. Traffic, weather and how long the vendor takes to prepare your order all affect them.

Can I cancel?

Free of charge before the vendor starts preparing. Once preparation has started, prepared and perishable items usually cannot be cancelled, because the vendor has already incurred the cost. If we cannot fulfil your order at all, you are not charged and anything already paid is refunded in full.

Something was missing or damaged. What now?

Reply in the same WhatsApp conversation as the order within 24 hours of delivery and quote your order number. We may ask for a photograph. Problems are resolved case by case, usually by refund or replacement.

Do I have to share my location?

No. Sharing a location pin is optional and there is always the option to continue with a typed address instead. When you do share one, it is attached to that order, used to help the rider find you, and not used to follow you between orders. Our privacy policy sets out the detail.

Which areas do you cover?

Badagry Town, Ajara, Ikoga, Age Mowo, Topo, Mowo, Seme, Imeke, Ibereko, Oko-Afo and Owode Apa. The menu page only shows areas that have a vendor actually delivering there, so what you see is what is genuinely available.

Sell locally? Ride locally?

We're adding vendors and riders across Badagry. Vendors get orders without building an app, and their menu goes live on our site the same day. Riders get jobs routed to their own zone.
